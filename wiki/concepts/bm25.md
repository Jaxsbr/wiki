# BM25 (Best Matching 25)

BM25 is a probabilistic ranking function and the default scorer in Elasticsearch, OpenSearch,
and most classical search engines. It succeeds precisely where vector embeddings fail: exact
and rare-token matching.

## Three Mechanisms

### 1. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)
Measures how rare a term is across the corpus. Common words ("service", "deployment") receive
low weight. Rare, distinguishing tokens ("v3.2", `ERR_PAYMENT_GATEWAY_TIMEOUT`, `payment_v2_enforce`)
receive high weight. This is why BM25 outperforms embeddings on exact-match queries — it most
heavily weights the tokens that actually distinguish one document from another.

### 2. Term Frequency (TF) Saturation
The first mention of a term significantly boosts the score; subsequent mentions yield
diminishing returns (score approaches a ceiling rather than growing linearly). Prevents
keyword-stuffed documents from gaming the ranking.

### 3. Length Normalisation
Corrects for the bias where longer documents score higher simply by having more words.
Relevance is measured by how often a term appears *relative to document length*, not
in absolute terms. Especially important when indexing variable-length chunks.

## When BM25 Wins

- Error codes pasted verbatim from logs
- Version strings (`v3.2`, `2019`)
- Named entities and identifiers that embeddings collapse into neighbourhoods
- Any query where the distinguishing information is a rare token

## When BM25 Loses

- Conceptual queries with no shared vocabulary (synonyms, paraphrases)
- Queries that use different words than the document for the same idea

## Relationship to Vector Search

BM25 and cosine similarity are complementary, not competing:

| | BM25 | Vector / Cosine |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Exact & rare-token matching | Semantic / conceptual matching |
| Scores | Unbounded | Bounded [−1, 1] |
| Normalisation | Not needed (rank-based fusion via [RRF](reciprocal-rank-fusion.md)) | Needed for direct combination |
