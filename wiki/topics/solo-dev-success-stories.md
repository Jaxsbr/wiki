# Solo & Micro-Team Steam Success Stories

20 documented cases. Focus: developers with average/basic starting skills who shipped something real.

→ Overview and strategy: [Solo Dev on Steam](solo-dev-on-steam.md)  
→ Scope rules: [First Game Scope Guide](../concepts/first-game-scope.md)

Revenue estimates from gamalytic/Steam Spy (±30-40% uncertainty) unless noted as confirmed.

## The Breakout Stories

These are the outliers — shown for inspiration and pattern extraction, not expectation-setting.

### Stardew Valley — ConcernedApe (Eric Barone), 2016

![Stardew Valley](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/413150/header.jpg)

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/413150/) · [Watch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Stardew+Valley+launch+trailer)

- **Revenue:** 50M+ units all platforms; est. $400M+ gross lifetime
- **Engine:** Custom C# / MonoGame (XNA framework)  
- **Art:** Self-taught 16-bit pixel art, inspired by SNES Harvest Moon
- **Dev time:** 4.5 years, 10–12 hours/day, 7 days/week
- **Prior skills:** CS degree (never got a job with it); no professional art, music, or game dev experience. Taught himself pixel art, music composition, and C# simultaneously
- **Day job:** Part-time evening usher at the Paramount Theatre; girlfriend worked two jobs supporting them
- **What worked:** Scratched an underserved itch (Harvest Moon on PC), obsessive polish, Chucklefish publishing handled porting/marketing

> "I felt like if I worked really hard for a few years I could make something great."

### Vampire Survivors — Luca Galante / poncle, 2022

![Vampire Survivors](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/1794680/header.jpg)

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/) · [Watch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Vampire+Survivors+official+trailer)

- **Revenue:** ~9.9M copies Steam; ~$57M gross; total asset cost ~£1,100
- **Engine:** Phaser (HTML5), later ported to Unity for v1.6+
- **Art:** Free sprite packs including a Castlevania-themed pack; chaotic visual mismatch was an accident — sprites weren't rendered at the right size; kept it because it worked
- **Dev time:** ~1 year (2020 → Dec 2021 Early Access)
- **Prior skills:** Programmer for a mobile gambling company; no indie game experience. "Mercenary" asset-grabbing approach
- **Day job:** Working at mobile gambling company when he started; quit within a week after Early Access exploded
- **What worked:** SplatterCat's YouTube video (Jan 7 2022) triggered a viral explosion; ultra-simple instantly-readable loop; $2.99 entry price; extremely streamable

### Balatro — LocalThunk, 2024

![Balatro](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/2379780/header.jpg)

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/) · [Watch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Balatro+game+official+trailer)

- **Revenue:** 5M+ copies; $50M+ gross; 119K copies on launch day alone
- **Engine:** LÖVE 2D (Lua-based framework)
- **Art:** Minimalist card UI — not artistically demanding
- **Dev time:** Dec 2021 prototype → Feb 2024 launch = ~2.25 years; first year was evenings/weekends only; quit IT job Jan 2023 to go full-time
- **Prior skills:** IT worker in Saskatchewan; had made hobby games for 10 years ("small projects shown to friends"). Intentionally avoided playing other deckbuilders: "I wanted to reinvent the wheel"
- **Day job:** Full-time IT worker until Jan 2023 — first entire year of development was nights and weekends
- **What worked:** Genuinely novel mechanic (poker + roguelite), Steam Next Fest demo drove 208K wishlists, organic streamer pickup

![Buckshot Roulette](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/2835570/header.jpg)

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2835570/) · [Watch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Buckshot+Roulette+official+trailer)

## The "Day Job Throughout" Cases

These are the most relevant for Jaco's situation.

### The First Tree — David Wehle, 2017

- **Revenue:** 200,000+ copies; ~$150K+ revenue
- **Engine:** Unity + **Playmaker** (visual scripting — **zero code written**)
- **Art:** Purchased Unity Asset Store packs (environment packages, templates); no original 3D art created
- **Dev time:** ~2 years, part-time while working full-time as technical artist at The VOID (VR company)
- **Prior skills:** Technical artist and video editor; **no programming knowledge whatsoever**
- **Day job:** Full-time technical artist throughout development
- **What worked:** Personal emotional narrative (lost father), short (~2 hours), $7.99 price, Reddit front page at launch, Firewatch-style marketing resonated

> "The only thing stopping you from making a game is yourself."

---

### Core Defense — Mario Kaiser (ehmprah), 2020

- **Revenue:** $20K first week; ~$50K–70K total
- **Engine:** Custom JavaScript (Vue.js + Pixi.js + Electron) — used familiar web dev tools, not Unity
- **Art:** Deliberately minimalist; no art skills needed — embraced it as a feature
- **Dev time:** Alpha in ~2 months; beta on itch.io 6 months before Steam launch; ~8 months total
- **Prior skills:** Web developer background; prior failed game (Coregrounds); no art skills
- **Day job:** Unknown; appears to have been indie dev after Coregrounds
- **What worked:** 3,000 wishlists from pre-release demo (best ROI of any tactic); Discord community during development; >50% sales from non-English markets after localization

## Pattern Summary Across All Cases

| Pattern | Games |
|---|---|
| Free asset packs → still huge revenue | Vampire Survivors, Brotato |
| Zero code / visual scripting | The First Tree (Playmaker), GameMaker drag-and-drop |
| Minimalist art as design choice | Core Defense, Luck Be a Landlord, Thronefall |
| Lo-fi 3D jank as aesthetic | Lethal Company, Buckshot Roulette |
| Personal emotional story | The First Tree, Stardew Valley, Undertale |
| Viral via one YouTube/TikTok video | Vampire Survivors (SplatterCat), Buckshot Roulette (TikTok) |
| Years of devlogs → wishlist mountain | Manor Lords (3M wishlists), Thronefall |
| itch.io → Steam pipeline | Buckshot Roulette, Core Defense, Brotato |
| Day job until near completion | LocalThunk (Balatro), Galante (Vampire Survivors), Wehle (First Tree) |
| 3 months or less | Buckshot Roulette, Terraria v1.0 (with caveats) |
